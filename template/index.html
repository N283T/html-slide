Background

A standard slide is a heading and a body

Everything else is a layout class away

This is layout-std: a .slide-head on top and a flexible .body below. Body text is 34px on a 1920×1080 canvas, which reads comfortably from the back of a seminar room.

  • Emphasis with strong for weight, .em for colour — keep the coloured one rare.
  • Asides and labels go in .muted grey, never body copy.
  • List items can appear step by step with data-fragment.
A .callout holds the one sentence you want remembered.
Sources or footnotes live in the .foot strip my-talk / 2026
